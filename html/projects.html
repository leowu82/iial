Misinformation on Social Media

Our research focuses on understanding the dynamics of misinformation on social media and digital platforms. We also explore innovative approaches to promote quality information and effective literacy programs to combat misinformation.

Library Services for Older Adults

Libraries serve diverse populations, including a rapidly expanding demographic of older adults. Our research investigates the needs of older adults and how libraries could meet their needs using information technologies. Also, we are dedicated to developing materials, tools, and systems for libraries and older adults for easy access of quality information.

Our recent IMLS National Leadership Project led by Dr. Jiangping Chen (PI) and Dr. Ping Li (Co-PI) will identify the information needs of older adults, examine current public library services for this population, and develop training materials for librarians as well as literacy courses for older adults.

Digital Libraries

We explore how digital objects can be organized and interconnected to serve users, multilingual digital collections and systems, the impact of AI on digital libraries, knowledge graph, and digital library users.

Our recent IMLS National Leadership Project led by Dr. Haihua Chen (PI) and Jiangping Chen (Co-PI) will evaluate the limitations of emerging multimodal AI systems in oral history contexts and develop a structured benchmark dataset and modular, open-source framework that supports cross-modal retrieval, evidence-grounded question answering, and narrative summarization while preserving archival integrity.
